HOW THE ENGINE WORKS

Every pick is explained

The engine assigns each match a model probability, compares it with a fair price, and ranks the difference. Only the strongest edges survive the filters.

01

It scores every option

Form, home advantage, matchup, surface and market price are combined into a single probability for each selection.

02

It finds the edge

Model probability is compared with the price on offer. A positive edge means the price is better than the engine believes it should be.

03

It filters by risk

Conservative keeps only the shortest, most likely selections. Aggressive allows longer prices and wider edges.
